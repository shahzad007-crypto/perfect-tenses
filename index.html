📐 PERFECT TENSE FORMULAS

1. Present Perfect (Past $\rightarrow$ Now)

Subject + have / has + Past Participle (V3)

Clues: since, for, just, already, yet, ever, never, recently

"She has lived in Ibra since 2021."

2. Past Perfect (Past Before Another Past)

Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)

Clues: before, after, by the time, when + Past Simple

"The train had left before we arrived at the station."

3. Future Perfect (Completed Before Future Deadline)

Subject + will have + Past Participle (V3)

Clues: by tomorrow, by next month, by 2030, within two years

"By next June, I will have graduated from UTAS."
